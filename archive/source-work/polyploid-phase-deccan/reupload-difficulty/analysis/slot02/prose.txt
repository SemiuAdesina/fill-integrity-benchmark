## What I built

Four modules under `/app/src/solver/`, with `solver.rs` reduced to wiring:

- **`tables.rs`** — flattens the cross into the arrays the inner loops need: per-marker admissible columns (≤6 each), transition matrices, combined double-reduction terms, legality masks.
- **`dp.rs`** — one progeny's path as Viterbi over the 100 joint states, factored into two 10×10 passes; plus forward/backward max-plus tables and the lexicographically smallest optimal path.
- **`canon.rs`** — canonical form, sorting permutations, and the ordering between distinct optima.
- **`search.rs`** — the global search.

## Why the search looks like this

The alternating step (paths given columns, columns given paths — each exact in its block) alone is useless here: 2000 random starts give 2000 different fixed points, the best of them ~2 nats below the optimum. Branch and bound is worse — the natural bound is ~117 nats loose.

So the descent sits inside a population search: a pool of 16 assignments, each round perturbing or crossing two of them marker by marker, then descending. Crossover is what makes it work — two assignments stuck for different reasons can share the markers they each got right.

I also added a neighbourhood that neither block step contains: replace the columns at one marker and re-optimise **every** path around it. The forward/backward tables bracket that marker and don't depend on its columns, so each candidate costs one max over 100 states. Running this and the alternating step to a point neither can move is what closed the gap — before it, roughly half of all runs fell short; with it, 8/8 seeds hit the optimum.

Where the space can be listed, two exact stages run as well: full enumeration when the joint space is small, and otherwise an alternation that optimises a whole parent at a time.

## Verification

- **Optimum**: `-705.140456203`, reached on 8/8 independent seeds; a 10× larger budget (2048 restarts, 200k rounds, 40k strong rounds, 170 s) found nothing better.
- **Brute force**: with the enumeration shortcuts disabled, 36 random crosses (spaces up to 46 656 assignment pairs) all matched the enumerated optimum.
- **Consistency**: the reported total is now the value of the paths actually written, not the Viterbi maximum — `delta = 0.0` against an independent Python scorer, and it can't drift by up to `N·eps` if paths tie.
- **Canonical / deterministic**: no canonical defect, digest matches, and repeated runs are byte-identical.
- **Tests**: `diagnostics` 4/4, `solver_checks` 6/6 (including `search_reaches_the_enumerated_optimum`).

Runtime is 18 s on the fixture and 20–27 s across the declared size range (budgets scale with `offspring × markers`, deterministically from the input — no wall-clock anywhere).

Two things worth flagging: I found and fixed a bug where `best_pair` only updated on a lexicographic comparison, so a strictly better score with a larger row key was silently discarded — that had been capping the search at `-725.2` regardless of seed. And the "global optimum" claim rests on the search rather than a proof: the evidence is unanimous agreement across seeds, budgets and brute-force checks, but for the largest instances (M=28, N=32 with all dosages at 2, a ~10²² space) it is a heuristic and I have no ground truth to check it against.
