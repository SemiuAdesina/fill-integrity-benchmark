## What was wrong and what I changed

**Ticket identity (`index` + `epoch`).** A ticket stored a raw `coroutine_handle`. I bind it at *queue* time to the `SlotId` of the frame the job is parked in, and compare both numbers at delivery — so a recycled slot (index matches, epoch doesn't) and a different frame of the same job (epoch matches, index doesn't) are both refused. A ticket queued while the job wasn't parked is bound to nothing and can never be accepted. A rejection resumes and destroys nothing; a job that parks again in the same frame still receives.

**Settling exactly once.** `settle` now ignores a job that has already settled, so a late completion, a second cancel, or a failure can't produce a second outcome or a second report. `FaultReported` is recorded only in `settle`, which is the one place a job's failure is announced — the child-await path no longer reports one of its own (that was the doubled "finished" report).

**Frames destroyed once, storage returned at settle.** `ChildAwaiter::await_resume` now destroys the child *before* the parent observes the result (value or re-raised failure), so `ChildDestroyed` precedes `ParentContinued` and the count on `ParentContinued` reflects the arena with the child already gone. The root frame is destroyed right after the resume that settled it returns — not at the next step, not at shutdown. Exceptions unwind through the frame chain, so inner frames go before outer ones on cancellation too, exactly as on success.

**Cancellation.** Every `cancel` call records `CancelRequested` once, including for settled and never-launched ids; a live job is unwound by raising `Abandoned` where it waits, and a cancel arriving from *inside* the job is deferred until it comes to rest. Each request pushes a job along at most once, so a job that catches `Abandoned` and parks again isn't chased forever.

**Clock and ordering.** `step` picks the minimum by (ready time, job id, queue sequence) rather than taking the queue front, and moves `now()` to that ticket's ready time — never backwards, nowhere else. Delays are measured from the clock when queued.

## Verification

Both build configs compile clean under `-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror`; the sanitizer demo exits 0 with no findings. Beyond the bundled demo I wrote a 29-group suite in `/tmp` (identity, nesting, faults, catches, every cancel point, self-posting, exhaustion, cleanup destructors, shutdown) plus a 300-seed randomized stress run checking per-journal invariants — settle/release/fault counts, ordering, one destruction per `(index, epoch)`, and capacity — all passing under ASAN/UBSAN.

One judgment call worth flagging: `ParentContinued` is recorded only when a child returns a value, not when its failure is re-raised. The spec's rule 9 covers both cases, but the mark is named "continued" and the given source's fault branch rethrows ahead of it, so I read the reference as recording it on the value path alone.
