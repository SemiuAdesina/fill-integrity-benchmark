## The fix

**`include/roundel/runtime.hpp`** — structural changes only; every public name and signature is intact:
- `ParkAwaiter::await_suspend` now returns `bool`: answering `false` means "don't suspend", so a job asked to stop *while it is running* never parks at all and abandons immediately instead.
- `Pending` carries `bound` + a `SlotId`; a ticket queued while the job wasn't parked is bound to nothing. `JobState` carries `parked_slot`, `released`, `fault_reported` and a resume `depth`.
- Added `cancel_requested()` / `report_fault()`, and private `resume_frame`, `unwind`, `release_settled`, `pump_cancels`.

**`src/runtime.cpp`**:
- **Frame binding**: a ticket is bound at queue time to the `(index, epoch)` pair of the one frame the job is parked in, and `deliver` accepts only if the job exists, hasn't settled, is parked, *and* both numbers match. Everything else is a rejection that touches nothing. This covers all three required rejections — settled/unlaunched, recycled slot (epoch differs), and a different frame of the same job (index differs) — while a job that re-parks in the same frame still receives its queued ticket.
- **Destruction**: `ChildAwaiter::await_resume` reads the child's result, destroys the child frame *first*, then records `ChildDestroyed` and either reports the fault and rethrows or records `ParentContinued` with the arena count at that instant. Root frames are destroyed by `release_settled()` as soon as the job settles (never while it is running), which cascades inner-first through the frames each one holds.
- **Settling once**: `settle()` is idempotent, forces `Cancelled` when a cancel was asked, and announces a failure at most once per job. Cancellation propagates as `Abandoned` through the frame stack, so a cancelled job never resumes, never produces a value, and reports no fault.
- **Cancellation**: every `cancel` call records `CancelRequested` once, for any identifier — including settled and never-launched ones. A parked job unwinds synchronously (storage back before the call returns); a running one is deferred to its next wait.
- **Scheduling**: `step` picks the minimum by (ready time, job id, queue sequence), moves the clock to that ready time and nowhere else, and `drain` repeats. Launching and posting from inside a running job keep working.

Beyond the bundled reproducer (all four scenarios now correct), I wrote five scratch harnesses in `/tmp` (not in `/app`): 223 assertions plus 400 randomised schedules checking journal invariants, arena counters, destruction order, delivery order and clock behaviour. Clean under gcc and clang, `-O0`/`-O2`, `-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror`, and under ASan and UBSan (leak detection included, which catches any slot left checked out — the arena deliberately leaks those).

Two notes: the `-fsanitize=address,undefined` combination intermittently dies before `main` in this sandbox even for programs with no roundel code, so I validated with each sanitizer separately as well. And where the spec admits more than one reading I followed the existing code's structure: `ParentContinued` is recorded on a child's returned value but not on a re-raised failure, and a job asked to stop settles `Cancelled` even if it swallows the `Abandoned`.
