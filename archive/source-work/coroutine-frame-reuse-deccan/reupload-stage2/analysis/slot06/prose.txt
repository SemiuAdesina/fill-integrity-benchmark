**How the guarantees are met:**
- **One settle, one release** — `settle()` ignores unknown/already-settled jobs; pushing to `pending_release_` and draining it right after every resume/launch/cancel/step means the frame is destroyed and the slot is back in the arena at the moment the job settles, not at shutdown.
- **Ticket binding** — `enqueue` binds a ticket to the `(index, epoch)` pair of the frame the job is parked in *at that instant*; a job that isn't parked authorises no frame. `deliver` compares the pair and rejects (touching nothing) on settled, recycled-slot, epoch-mismatch and index-mismatch; a valid re-park in the same frame still receives it.
- **Cancellation** — records `CancelRequested` once per call for any id, including unknown and settled ones; for a parked job it resumes the frame once so cleanup runs, then settles `Cancelled` if it didn't on its own; for a running job it unwinds at the next park. Idempotent and total.
- **Child frames** — the parent promise owns the child handle; `await_resume` destroys the child (recording `ChildDestroyed`) *before* returning a value or re-raising the fault. `destroy_stack` walks parent links innermost-first and clears the owning link so nothing is destroyed twice.
- **Ordering/clock** — `step` picks min by (ready, job, seq), advances the clock to that ticket's ready time only, and delivers exactly one ticket.
