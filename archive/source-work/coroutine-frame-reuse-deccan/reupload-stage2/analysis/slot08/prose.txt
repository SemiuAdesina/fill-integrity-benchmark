## Root causes

**Frame identity.** `Pending::target` held a raw `coroutine_handle` — an address. Once a slot was recycled, that address named a different frame, so a ticket woke whatever now lived there. Tickets now carry the `(index, epoch)` pair captured at queue time plus a `bound` flag; delivery compares **both** numbers against the frame the job is parked in, and a ticket queued for an unparked job is bound to nothing and can never be accepted.

**Cancellation.** `cancel` flagged the parked frame and resumed it so `await_resume` would throw. That resumes a frame, lets a job that catches `Abandoned` carry on, and re-enters frames whose slots may be gone. Cancel now destroys the frame stack directly (innermost first), which also runs the frames' cleanup and returns storage at once. A request made from *inside* the job is recorded and acted on at the checkpoint after the resume returns — the frames can't be destroyed while they're on the stack.

**Frame ownership.** The child's frame was owned inconsistently: leaked on the success path, destroyed-without-detaching on the fault path. The conductor now owns every frame of a job in a stack; `ChildAwaiter::await_resume` destroys the child and records `ChildDestroyed` *before* `ParentContinued` (so the arena count on `ParentContinued` is taken after the slot went back), on both the value and failure paths.

**Settling and release.** `settle()` had no once-guard (two `JobSettled`, two `FaultReported`), release used a single `pending_release_` field that got overwritten (frames left alive), and `cancel` re-settled an already-settled job. `settle` now returns early if the job has settled, `FaultReported` is recorded exactly once at settle, and release is queued per job and run to a fixpoint right after each resume. `CancelRequested` is recorded once per call, including for unknown and already-settled jobs.

**Ordering and the clock.** `step` delivered `queue_.front()` and did `++tick_`. It now picks the minimum by (ready time, job id, queue sequence) and moves the clock only to the delivered ticket's ready time.

## Verification

- The four demo scenarios now report correctly (slot released at cancel and reused with a new epoch; one settle; `ChildDestroyed`/`ParentContinued` with the child already gone; `live=0` at the end, `double_reclaims=0`).
- Four hand-written harnesses (317 assertions) plus a randomized stress harness: **653k invariant checks over 400 runs**, zero failures — settle-once, release-once, `JobSettled` before `FrameReleased`, no double reclaim, clock monotonic, `live <= capacity`.
- Zero ASan/UBSan findings and no leaks (LeakSanitizer confirmed active), clean under `-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror` with both GCC 12 and Clang 17.
